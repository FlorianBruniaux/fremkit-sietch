# Changelog

What changed for the person using this widget, version by version. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); the newest version is first, and the
entry of the version being published is what the Fremkit admin shows before an update.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-16

### Added
- First release on the registry. The tokens [RTK](https://github.com/rtk-ai/rtk) saved today and
  its savings rate, the overall total, and one bar per day over the last 7, 14 or 30 days. Needs a
  Fremkit with the `rtk` channel (after 0.3.0) and RTK installed on the Mac.
